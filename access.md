# Access

Public access is anticipated in 2027.

## Collaborators

Collaborators outside the University of Oregon receive a copy of the dataset
through [Globus](https://www.globus.org), with permission from J. Benjamin
Hutchinson ([bhutch@uoregon.edu](mailto:bhutch@uoregon.edu)). Lab members at
the University of Oregon work with the dataset in place on the university's
cluster and do not need a copy.

To request a copy, email with your name, institution and what you plan to use
the data for. You will need a Globus account and a Globus collection on the
system where the copy will live; most university research clusters provide
one.

Copies shared with collaborators have defaced anatomical images and contain no
recordings of participants' voices. A future public release will go through
further de-identification.

To work with your copy through Claude, see
[Claude and the MCP Server](https://hulacon.github.io/mmmdata/doc/claude-and-mcp).
